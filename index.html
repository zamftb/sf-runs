Brad Zamft's LinkedIn Profile

Brad Zamft is the founder and CEO of Heritable Agriculture, a company combining AI, environmentally-informed breeding, and gene editing to accelerate crop development. He conceived and led the project at X, Alphabet's "Moonshot Factory," before it spun out independently. Brad holds a PhD in Physics from UC Berkeley and completed postdoctoral research in synthetic biology at Harvard Medical School. He previously served as Chief Scientific Officer at TL Biolabs, focused on DNA diagnostics for cattle, and managed grant programs at the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and the U.S. Department of Energy's ARPA-E.

Heritable Agriculture

Heritable Agriculture is the company Brad co-founded and serves as CEO of, an agricultural biotechnology startup making plants programmable. By combining artificial intelligence, environmentally-informed breeding, and gene editing, Heritable accelerates how quickly new crop traits can be developed, cutting the cost and time typically required to bring genetic improvements to market. The project began inside X, Alphabet's "Moonshot Factory," before spinning out as an independent company. The team applies synthetic biology techniques at scale to help transform how the world grows food, targeting real-world agricultural challenges with precise genetic programming.

Zamft Tutoring

Zamft Tutoring, founded and led by Brad's father, Hy Zamft, provides personalized academic support for students facing challenges that make traditional schooling difficult. With over 25 years of experience, its team of state-certified teachers offers homebound instruction and one-on-one tutoring tailored to each student's needs, including cases other agencies won't take. The practice partners closely with schools and families to keep students on track, building both skills and confidence. Based in South Salem, New York, Zamft Tutoring has helped generations of students overcome obstacles and reach their academic potential.

Eric Zamft's LinkedIn Profile

Eric Zamft is Brad's brother and the Director of Planning for the City of Cleveland Heights, Ohio, a role he's held since early 2021. He oversees zoning, land use, transportation planning, economic development, and comprehensive and master plan preparation for the city. Eric holds a master's degree in City Planning – Land Use and Transportation from the University of Pennsylvania and is certified by the American Institute of Certified Planners. Before Cleveland Heights, he spent five years as a planning and economic development director in Port Chester, New York, building a career in municipal planning dating back to internships in 1996.
